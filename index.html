PhD Candidate in Computer Science, George Mason University

Suzzana Rafi

My current research is focused on Software Testing and Analysis, particularly on enhancing testing techniques through the use of AI techniques.

I have experience working on detecting, repairing and reproducing flaky tests, which are tests that pass and fail non-deterministically on the same version of code, and have built heuristics and large language model based tools to address testing problems.
